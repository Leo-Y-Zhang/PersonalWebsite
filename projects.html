Method

The through-line: claims are cheap, verification is the work.

  • Tests that were seen to fail. New behaviour starts with a failing test; a test never observed failing is decoration. Mutation testing guards the safety-critical paths.
  • Generated, never typed. Documents that quote numbers are derived from the systems they describe, with drift gates that fail when document and code disagree.
  • Pre-registration. Research declares hypotheses and thresholds before it runs; a result that fails its gate is published as a failure, not re-tuned until it passes.
  • Security as a floor. Secret scanning on every commit across every repository, dependency auditing, and periodic security sweeps with adversarial verification of every finding.
  • Honest limits. Solver verdicts are not proofs, a private repository's figures are dated measurements, and unfinished work is labelled unfinished.
